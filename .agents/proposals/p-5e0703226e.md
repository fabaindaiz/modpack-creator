# Proposal: decision-review

Counted the owner's answers to recommended options in two kinds of one-per-turn question in the same carrier: decisions on a design, and the pick of harvest candidates, where some recommendations were to keep a candidate local or not propose it.

## Result

In design decisions the owner took the recommended option in about fifty of fifty-two answers, as the target proposal states. In the harvest pick of the period's first day, the owner took all nine recommendations to propose and overrode all four recommendations not to propose, keeping each one (as evidence, as a figure, marking a source). The boundary moves: the near-total acceptance holds for recommendations that add or build, not for recommendations to drop, so a recommendation to discard is still put to the owner and never decided alone. This is a second count for the harvest method's step that tells the agent not to choose alone.
