# Proposal: clean-context-review-closes-a-batch

A review in a clean context is part of closing a batch of work, not something offered. In the carrier's measured practice, the reviewer found at least one important or critical defect that neither the author nor the delegated agents' reports had seen, and each fix was made with its test seen to fail first. The close of a batch therefore names the review as a step with its range and its findings, and a branch closed without one says so as a gap in the record.

## Evidence

One carrier, one owner, with a contrast from two other carriers of that owner, where the step was not done and the branches were closed unreviewed. In the first carrier, at the close of each batch, about six of six clean-context reviews found at least one important or critical finding: two critical and more than a dozen important in total, for example an operation that could silently replace an installed item with an older one, a stored record rebuilt by every write path so that a field would empty, and a digest that marked every record stale after a deploy. The gate was green in each case and the fixes were each preceded by a failing test. The two batches that skipped the review were fixes for an earlier review's findings, and were noted in the log as unreviewed. Literature: the candidate's own; no new source consulted.
