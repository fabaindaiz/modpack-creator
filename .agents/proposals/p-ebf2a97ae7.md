# Proposal: ready-is-stated-in-the-humans-terms

A report that says only that something is ready makes the human ask again; it says which parts of the request are done, which are not, and what still waits (merge, push, a check against the real system).

## Evidence

One carrier, 2026-10-12 to 2026-10-14: the human asked whether the work was ready, what was left or how long it would take eight times in three days, and once expected as done a whole sub-feature that had never been built. Offered as a figure only: the rule restates the owner's own global instruction file, so the agreement measures that file's reach, not the rule's generality.
